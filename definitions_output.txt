FORMULAS (positive output means later than the comparison time)

B history: B = t - [scheduled_start + f * median(historical observed route runtime)]. Needs A plus comparable completed historical runtimes; unavailable where history is missing.
C promise: C = median over not-yet-passed booked stops of [t - promised_eta]. Needs a received ping, stop position, and at least one upcoming booking.
D speed: D = [t + remaining_polyline_km/current_speed_kmph*60] - scheduled_end. Needs a received ping, valid positive speed, and a speed representative of future travel.
