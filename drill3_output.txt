ASSUMPTIONS
1. Timestamps are explicit IST values.
2. Trip pings share vehicle_id and have recorded_at within exact scheduled bounds.
3. Direction-aware fraction uses origin-to-destination geometry for inbound, and 100 minus it for outbound.
4. Near terminus means <=100m from first or last listed stop; slow/stationary means speed <=1 km/h.
5. Five-minute samples select the last recorded ping in each 5-minute bucket; no interpolation.
6. No reconciliation rule, exclusion, lateness metric, or operational verdict is applied.
