ASSUMPTIONS
1. Timestamps are parsed as explicit IST offsets.
2. A trip ping uses same vehicle plus recorded_at inside exact scheduled start/end.
3. Near stop/origin/destination means <=100m; moving means speed >1 km/h.
4. GPS progress is a projection onto the ordered stop polyline using a local planar approximation.
5. Scheduled-time fraction assumes uniform progress from 0% at start to 100% at end, including no explicit dwell allocation.
6. Fixed-clock comparisons use 07:45 IST and last ping received by that time unless noted.
7. Stop arrival is first ping within 100m of that stop, not proof of a physical stop.
8. No row is excluded, corrected, or used to calculate lateness.

1. POLYLINE FRACTION VS SCHEDULED-TIME FRACTION
Polyline fraction is GPS location projected onto stop geometry / polyline length. Scheduled-time fraction is (time - scheduled_start) / (scheduled_end - scheduled_start). The latter is a straight-line-in-time assumption: at 50% of scheduled time, the bus is assumed 50% along the polyline. It has no dwell-time information.
