
====================================================================================================
ASSUMPTIONS AND SCOPE
====================================================================================================
1. Every offset-bearing timestamp is parsed with %Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S%z and normalized to Asia/Kolkata.
2. 'Mumbai bounds' means latitude 18.8 to 19.6 and longitude 72.7 to 73.3; it is a coarse data-quality screen.
3. Reported speed >120 km/h or <0, and coordinate-implied speed >130 km/h, are flagged as implausible, not proved wrong.
4. A ping is assigned to a trip only when its vehicle matches and recorded_at is inside that trip's scheduled window. This is for description, not a lateness model.
5. 'Far from route' means >300 m from the stop polyline; sub-100m geometry is explicitly not over-interpreted.
6. A stationary stretch is consecutive pings with reported speed <=1 km/h and movement <=25 m, lasting >=5 minutes.
7. Boarding-stop scheduled time is a linear interpolation by stop-polyline distance; this is only a schedule consistency check.
8. 'Future' is reported both relative to the run clock and relative to the requested 07:45 snapshot. Neither is a claim of bad data.
9. No rows are filtered, corrected, excluded, or used to calculate lateness. Operator 7 and Route 12 remain included everywhere.
