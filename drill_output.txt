ASSUMPTIONS
1. All timestamps are parsed as offset-bearing IST timestamps.
2. Trip ping membership means same vehicle and recorded_at within exact scheduled start/end.
3. 'Near origin/destination/stop' means within 100m of that stop; 'moving' means speed_kmph > 1.
4. Route progress is projection onto the ordered stop polyline using a local planar approximation; it is descriptive, not road-matched.
5. Timetable progress fraction is elapsed scheduled time / scheduled runtime. It is not a lateness calculation.
6. A lag episode is defined here as lag >=300 seconds; a '90-minute' episode is exactly 5400 seconds.
7. 'Last ping before silence' means the final recorded_at ping before 07:45; no cause is inferred from absence of later pings.
8. Completed-trip runtime requires both a moving ping within 100m of origin and a ping within 100m of destination; otherwise it is reported unavailable.
9. No operator, route, ping, or booking is excluded or corrected; no reconciliation rule is applied.
